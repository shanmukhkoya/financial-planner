# 📘 Financial Planner - User Guide

Welcome to your new Financial Planner! This application is designed to be more than just a tracker—it's a tool to help you forecast your future and make smarter decisions today.

Here is how to get the best out of every feature.

---

## 1. 🏠 The Dashboard: Your Financial Command Center

The dashboard gives you an instant snapshot of your financial health.

*   **Current Balance**: Your real-time net worth (Cash in hand).
*   **Monthly Flow**:
    *   <span style="color: #34d399">Green</span>: Your average monthly income.
    *   <span style="color: #f87171">Red</span>: Your average monthly expenses.
    *   **Tip**: Keep the gap between these two numbers as wide as possible!
*   **Forecast Chart**: The blue area chart shows your projected balance for the next 6 months.

---

## 2. 💸 Transactions: Track to Optimize

Don't just log expenses; categorize them smarty to get better insights.

### How to use it best:
*   **Be Consistent**: Log expenses daily. It takes seconds.
*   **Categories Matter**: Use consistent category names like "Food", "Rent", "EMI", "Shopping". The system uses these to generate alerts.
*   **Income**: Don't forget to log your Salary on the 1st of the month!

---

## 3. 💣 Debt Manager: visually Attack Your Loans

The Debt section helps you visualize the burden of your loans and plan their repayment.

### Best Strategy:
1.  **Add All Debts**: Home loans, credit cards, personal loans.
2.  **Watch the Progress Bar**: The purple bar shows how much you've paid off.
3.  **Prioritize**:
    *   Look at the **Interest Rate**. Use this app to identify which debt is costing you the most (usually Credit Cards).
    *   Target the high-interest debt first!

---

## 4. 🔮 Forecast: The "Crystal Ball" Feature

**This is the most powerful feature.** Most apps tell you what happened *yesterday*. This app tells you what will happen *tomorrow*.

### How to use it:
*   **Plan Ahead**: Know you have an insurance premium coming up in 3 months?
*   **Add an Event**: Go to "Plan Future Expense".
    *   *Name*: Car Insurance
    *   *Date*: 3 months from now
    *   *Amount*: ₹15,000
    *   *Recurring*: Yearly
*   **See the Impact**: Look at the chart immediately. Did your projected balance dip below zero?
    *   **If YES**: You have 3 months to save extra or cut costs. You have been warned in advance!

---

## 5. 💡 Smart Insights

The app analyzes your spending against typical budget limits.

*   **Green Alerts**: You are doing great!
*   **Orange/Red Alerts**: You are overspending in a category (e.g., "You have exceeded your Food budget").
*   **Action**: If you see a red alert for "Eating Out", try cooking at home for the rest of the month to get back on track.

---

## Summary of Workflow
1.  **Daily**: Log transactions.
2.  **Weekly**: Check the Dashboard Insights.
3.  **Monthly**: Update Debt balances (if you paid some off externally) and add upcoming big expenses to the Forecast.

---

## 6. 🧹 Managing Data (Advanced)

If you want to start fresh or remove all data:

1.  Open your terminal in the `financial-planner/server` directory.
2.  Run the command:
    ```bash
    npm run reset
    ```
3.  **Refresh your browser** to see the clean slate.

Start taking control of your financial future today! 🚀
